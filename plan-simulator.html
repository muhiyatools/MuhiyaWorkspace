Win / Win Checklist

How the math works. A plan's budget is denominated at your charged model prices, so its true worst-case cost to you is budget ÷ markup. A plan is worst-case safe when budget ÷ markup ≤ price × (1 − fees) — even a user who burns 100% of their credits can't put you underwater. Typical utilization is far lower, which is where margin lives. Burst (5h) windows protect against leaked keys and abuse spikes; the free plan's weekly window stops a new account burning a whole month in one night.
